01 / THE HEART OF CASITA LIA

A little home.

Created for the people who matter most.

Casita Lia began with a simple dream: a warm, welcoming space where families, friends, and couples can slow down, reconnect, and make beautiful memories together.

“Casita” means “little house.” Lia is our daughter and the first granddaughter in our family, who has brought so much joy, love, and warmth into our lives.

Just as she holds a special place in our family, we hope this cozy, thoughtfully designed home becomes a special little place in yours. For coffee and conversations, movie nights and meaningful moments. For some of your happiest memories.

House rules.

Make yourself at home and please treat Casita Lia with the same care you would your own.

Up to 8 registered guests

A smoke-free stay

Check-in & check-out

Check-in is from 3:00 PM; check-out is at 12:00 NN. Early check-in and late check-out require prior approval and are subject to availability.

Guests & visitors
  • Maximum of 8 guests.
  • Only registered guests may stay overnight.
  • Inform the host in advance about visitors.
  • Parties and large gatherings are not allowed.

Small, intimate gatherings may be permitted when the guest limit and noise policies are respected. Please discuss your plans with the host beforehand.

Keep it cozy

Keep noise at a respectful level, especially in the evening. Please be considerate of neighbors and other condominium residents.

No smoking or vaping

Smoking and vaping are prohibited inside the unit and on the balcony.

Clean as you go

Please wash used dishes, dispose of trash properly, and keep the space reasonably tidy. No deep cleaning is required.

Smart lock & security
  1. Read the access guide and check-in instructions sent before your arrival.
  2. Use the PIN provided by the host to enter through the smart lock.
  3. Keep your access details private and make sure the door is securely locked whenever you leave.
  4. Contact the host if you need help with access.

The building has 24/7 security. Complete instructions specific to the lock will be included in your guest access guide.

Kitchen

Cooking is welcome in the fully equipped kitchen.

Take care of our home

Please handle furniture, appliances, décor, linens, and other items with care. Damage, missing items, or excessive cleaning may result in additional charges.

Linens & towels

Please do not use white towels or linens for makeup, spills, or shoes. Stained white linens requiring special cleaning may be subject to a ₱200 cleaning fee.

Building amenities

Swimming pool access is ₱200 per person. Building amenities remain subject to condominium rules and availability.

Before you leave
  • Switch off lights, air conditioners, and appliances.
  • Wash used kitchenware and dispose of trash.
  • Check that you have all your personal belongings.
  • Secure the smart lock before leaving.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you might want to know before your stay at Casita Lia.

How many guests can stay?

Casita Lia accommodates up to 8 guests across 3 bedrooms. All 3 bedrooms and the living room are air-conditioned, and the unit has a private balcony.

When can we check in and out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 NN. Self check-in is available through a smart lock; your PIN and access instructions will be sent before arrival. Early check-in and late check-out require advance confirmation and depend on availability.

Is parking available?

Yes. Parking is ₱100 per night and should be arranged with your host in advance.

Can we use the swimming pool?

Yes. Pool access is ₱200 per person, subject to condominium rules and availability.

Is there Wi-Fi and Netflix?

Yes. High-speed Wi-Fi and a Smart TV with Netflix are included, whether you're catching up on work or settling in for a movie night.

Can we cook? Is coffee provided?

Yes. The fully equipped kitchen includes a refrigerator, microwave, multi-cooker, electric kettle, cookware, dishes, and utensils. The dedicated coffee nook has a coffee maker and complimentary brewed coffee.

Are toiletries, linens, and towels included?

Yes. Bed linens, towels, shampoo, conditioner, and body wash are provided. The bathroom has a hot and cold shower.

Are visitors or small gatherings allowed?

Please disclose visitors to the host in advance. Only registered guests can stay overnight.

Small, intimate gatherings may be permitted when the maximum guest limit and noise policies are respected. Large parties and disruptive events are prohibited. Please discuss your plans with the host before booking.

Is smoking allowed on the balcony?

No. Smoking and vaping are prohibited both inside the unit and on the private balcony.

Where is Casita Lia, and how far is NAIA?

We're at The Rochester Condominium, Parklane Tower, San Joaquin, Pasig City. NAIA is approximately 20–30 minutes away by car, depending on traffic, route, and time of day.
