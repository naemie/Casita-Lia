How do I book Casita Lia?

Use “Book Your Stay” to share your preferred dates and party size. We’ll confirm the details and next steps with you directly.

What is included in the condo?

Your stay includes a full kitchen, Wi‑Fi, air conditioning, smart entertainment, bedroom linens, and building amenities as available for your dates.

Is the building secure?

Yes. Casita Lia is located within a secure condominium building, designed to make arrivals and everyday comings-and-goings feel easy.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Of course. Open the booking form with your question and preferred dates, and we will respond with the information you need.
